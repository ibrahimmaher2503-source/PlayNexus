# Administration visual audit

Date: 2026-09-13  
Scope: the 72 administration PNGs registered by the full-project QA manifests for `assignments`, `audit`, `branches-manage`, `branch-settings`, `roles`, `staff`, `staff-create`, `tenant`, and `tenant-settings`.

## Evidence and method

- Viewed every requested PNG with `view_image`: 54 main-manifest captures (desktop/tablet/mobile, Arabic/English) plus 18 supplemental `tablet1024` captures.
- Read the targeted baseline before judging the captures: `docs/01-PRD-Baseline.md`, `docs/08-Permission-Matrix.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and the top of `docs/agent-plan.md`.
- The manifests record route, identity, locale, viewport, HTTP status, console/page errors, overflow, and result. They do not record `goal` or `action`; those two registry fields below are audit labels derived from the route and the PRD flow, not claims that an interaction was executed.
- All 72 records are owner captures and are marked `PASS`/HTTP 200 with no console or page errors. This is static-render evidence only. Keyboard/focus order, form submission, validation, permission enforcement, dynamic filtering, and post-action state are untested here.
- No guardian phone, QR, safety note, or family list appears in the requested administration captures. Staff email addresses are visible in the owner-only staff/profile views, consistent with the targeted permission baseline. Static images cannot prove tenant isolation or dynamic list scoping, so those remain untested rather than claimed.

## Global overflow guard

The two manifests contain 218 PNG records in total and exactly 10 records with `overflow=true`. This audit does not change that global total. The administration subset contributes 2/72 of those records: `branch-settings__ar__mobile.png` and `branch-settings__en__mobile.png`. The other eight global overflow records belong to non-administration captures. The registry therefore preserves the required global total of **10**, rather than treating this administration slice as a new global count.

## Deduplicated findings

### ADM-01 — Responsive administration tables lose required data or actions (P1)

Observed in the images: the audit table is clipped at 820px and 1024px and collapses to only event/actor on mobile with no visible details/expand control; the branch-management table clips the right-side management copy on mobile; opening-hours tables clip the `Closes at` column on mobile; staff mobile rows expose only Name/Email and hide current status plus the status selector/save action; tenant-profile mobile rows expose only Name/Email and hide Status. The two branch-settings mobile captures also carry `overflow=true` in the manifest.

Impact: an owner cannot reliably compare the full audit record, see all branch-management consequences, edit both opening-hour times, change staff status, or verify staff status from a narrow device. This is an information/action loss, not merely a cosmetic crop. It conflicts with the design requirement to keep required decision columns and move secondary data to a visible expandable/detail surface.

Exact proposal: at `max-width: 430px`, render each administrative row as a labeled stacked card (or an explicit disclosure row) with every required field and action in task order. For opening hours, stack Day, Day state, Opens at, and Closes at; for staff, retain Current status, New status, and Save status; for tenant profile, retain Status; for audit, expose all fields through stacked labeled details or a keyboard-accessible “View details” control. At 820/1024px keep the full table inside the content column or make its overflow container visibly scrollable with the first/last column reachable; do not let text disappear behind a clipped edge. Preserve RTL field order, 44px controls, and bidirectional isolation for codes/times.

Testable acceptance: at 390x844, 430x932, 820x1180, and 1024x768 in both locales, no required field/action is outside the viewport or hidden without a labeled disclosure. A screenshot/DOM assertion can find the audit action, subject, outcome, reason, before, and after; branch settings can find both time inputs; staff can find status and Save status for each non-owner; tenant profile can find each staff status. Page-level horizontal overflow is zero, and keyboard focus reaches every revealed field/action in logical order.

Priority: P1 because the narrow administration surfaces are incomplete for the stated management tasks. The exact visual fix is intentionally left to the UI owner; no code was changed in this audit.

### ADM-02 — 1024px navigation footer occludes lower administration links (P1)

Observed in all 18 supplemental `tablet1024` captures: the expanded sidebar remains in desktop mode while the fixed organization footer begins over the lower navigation region. Depending on the page, the final navigation item is cut to a sliver or is not reachable below the footer. This is visible even though the manifest correctly reports no page-level horizontal overflow.

Impact: at the documented tablet boundary an owner can lose access to lower management destinations such as Audit log; the active route may be visible while adjacent administration routes are visually and interactively occluded.

Exact proposal: reserve a scrollable sidebar-navigation region above the fixed organization footer, or move the navigation to the drawer breakpoint at 1024px after design sign-off. Ensure the footer never overlays a nav item; keep the active item and the final Audit log item fully visible, with a visible focus target and no clipping.

Testable acceptance: every supplemental 1024px capture has all sidebar links fully visible or reachable by sidebar scroll/drawer, including the final administration link; footer and nav do not overlap; keyboard focus can reach each link; Arabic and English retain the same reachability. Re-run the 18 supplemental captures and keep global `overflow=true` at 10/218 unless a separately approved registry update changes it.

Priority: P1 because it blocks navigation to an administration surface at a supported viewport. No code or runtime was changed in this audit.
